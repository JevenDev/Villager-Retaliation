# Reputation

Reputation is stored for each player-villager relationship. Two villagers in the same village can feel very differently about you. Direct events, witnesses, gossip, persistent history, and optional decay all contribute.

## Default tiers

- **Royalty:** 1000 or higher.
- **Revered:** 400 or higher.
- **Respected:** 250 or higher.
- **Trusted:** 75 or higher.
- **Neutral:** -74 through 74.
- **Suspicious:** -75 or lower.
- **Hostile:** -100 or lower.
- **Despised:** -400 or lower.
- **Feared:** -1000 or lower.

Servers may change every threshold. Low standing can worsen prices and dialogue, block services or quests, extend anger, make pacification harder, and contribute to fleeing or aggression. Short combat anger can expire while the longer reputation damage remains.

## Recovering trust

- Trade with villagers who still allow it.
- Give liked or loved gifts without repeatedly farming the same item.
- Heal or save villagers and defend their village from real threats.
- Complete quests and honor their turn-in requirements.
- Stop opening watched property and allow suspicion to cool.

## Pacification

Hostile villagers can accept a datapack-defined payment only while their tier and server rules permit it. The built-in payment is **3–32** :item[minecraft:emerald].

:::notice{type="danger"}
Pacification is not an eraser. Severe personal history can remain after immediate hostility ends, and the lowest tiers may refuse peaceful options entirely.
:::

See [Watched property](../social/watched-property.md) for the easiest way to avoid accidental reputation loss.
