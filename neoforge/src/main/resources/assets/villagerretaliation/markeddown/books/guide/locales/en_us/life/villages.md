# Villages & Allegiance

Tracked villages have durable identities, generated names, resident rosters, and footprints built from points of interest, structure pieces, and connected terrain. A villager's home is not simply the closest village right now.

- Villagers born or created inside a tracked footprint join that village.
- Villagers created outside one begin as **Wanderers**.
- A Wanderer who remains in a village for one Minecraft day settles there; active party villagers do not auto-settle.
- Same-party and same-village allegiance prevents friendly targeting and affects community defense.

## Reassigning home

The interaction screen's **Home** topic identifies where a villager belongs. A player who is Revered or Royalty with that villager can ask them to adopt the current active village, then confirm the request. A recruited villager accepts only from a qualifying player in the same party.

## Naming a village

1. Hold a banner-it is not consumed.
2. Right-click a :item[minecraft:bell] inside the tracked village.
3. Be Revered or Royalty with at least half of its tracked living adult residents.
4. Enter a unique 1–32 character name without formatting or control codes.

:::notice{type="info"}
Village footprints can merge or change with their evidence. This is not a player-painted land-claim system.
:::
