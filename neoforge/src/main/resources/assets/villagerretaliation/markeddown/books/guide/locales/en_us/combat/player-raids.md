# Player Raids

Wear an ominous banner as a helmet inside a tracked village and begin using a :item[minecraft:goat_horn]. The initiator and current party are snapshotted as raiders. The raid cannot overlap a vanilla raid, conflicting Player Raid, or village cooldown.

:::notice{type="danger"}
This is deliberate betrayal. Recruited residents of the target village permanently leave the raiding party, confront the initiator, and rejoin their neighbors. Target-village residents apply severe reputation consequences toward every raider.
:::

## Defense

- Preparation lasts 10 seconds by default.
- Adult non-nitwits fill empty equipment slots with militia gear.
- Babies and nitwits hide while capable adults fight.
- Aligned iron golems arrive in budgeted reinforcement waves.
- Reusing the banner-and-horn interaction reveals nearby tracked defenders.
- Only the start snapshot counts; later births and visitors are not objectives, and golems never count as remaining villagers.

## Mercy and outcomes

After armed defenders are resolved, snapshotted babies and nitwits enter a mercy stage. Raiders may right-click to spare each survivor or leave the outcome unresolved. Spared villagers survive with exactly **-1000 reputation** toward every raider.

Raiders win after defender and mercy outcomes are resolved. The village wins if no living, non-spectator raider remains inside its footprint for 30 seconds by default. Either completed outcome applies a default three-Minecraft-day cooldown.
