# Hired Work

Adult villagers can take paid contracts for practical work. Hired work is separate from [party recruitment](party/_index.md): a villager cannot be hired and recruited at the same time.

1. Inspect **Job Stats** to compare role aptitude and performance.
2. Buy a 1–30 day contract from the interaction screen.
3. Use a :item[villagerretaliation:clipboard] to assign the work area or route and any required storage.
4. Supply tools, stations, inputs, and output space.
5. Read the worker's current state when something stops.

Continue with [Hiring and contracts](work/hiring.md), [Clipboard and storage](work/clipboard-storage.md), [Jobs](work/jobs.md), or [Builder and blueprints](work/builder.md).
