# Gifts & Skill Trades

## Gift basics

Villagers judge gifts using global rules and then profession preferences. Reactions are **Loved**, **Liked**, **Neutral**, **Disliked**, or **Hated**. The entire gifted stack must fit in the villager's inventory.

Reliable positive gifts include :item[minecraft:emerald], :item[minecraft:diamond], :item[minecraft:gold_ingot], :item[minecraft:golden_apple], bread, fruit, safe food, and useful village goods. Profession tools and materials are often even better.

Hazards and rotten loot-including TNT, lava, fire-starting items, rotten flesh, poisonous food, and ominous items-are usually disliked or hated.

:::details{id="profession-examples" title="Profession gift examples"}
- Armorers value metal, armor, shields, anvils, and blast furnaces.
- Farmers value crops, seeds, hoes, composters, and food.
- Librarians value books, paper, ink, maps, compasses, and lecterns.
- Masons value clay, brick, stone, quartz, copper, and stonecutters.
- Toolsmiths and weaponsmiths value quality tools, weapons, metal, and smithing supplies.
- Nitwits prefer treats, music discs, decorations, flowers, and playful items.
:::

By default, another stack of the same positive item on the same Minecraft day earns only **10%** reputation, and positive gift reputation is capped at **120 per relationship per day**. Negative reactions keep their full penalty.

At Revered and Royalty standing, villagers can offer profession-themed keepsakes or high-trust rewards.

## Skill trades and Special Orders

Skill trades are generated from profession, individual skill scores, trade level, reputation gates, and server settings. High-reputation **Special Orders** let you directly request eligible unlocked definitions.

:::notice{type="info"}
Not every villager of a profession has the same skill trades. Skills are individual, and server datapacks can replace the available definitions.
:::

See [Skills, attributes, and care](../life/skills-care.md) for how those scores work.
