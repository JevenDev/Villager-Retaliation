# Advancements

Villager Retaliation includes a dedicated reputation advancement tab covering first impressions, trade, gifts, stories, crime, pacification, combat, village defense, trust, and the consequences of extreme choices.

Open the tab through :advancement-group[villagerretaliation:reputation/root]. Useful milestones include:

- :advancement[villagerretaliation:reputation/familiar_face]
- :advancement[villagerretaliation:reputation/friend_of_the_village]
- :advancement[villagerretaliation:reputation/regular_customer]
- :advancement[villagerretaliation:reputation/peace_offering]
- :advancement[villagerretaliation:reputation/story_keeper]
- :advancement[villagerretaliation:reputation/local_legend]
- :advancement[villagerretaliation:reputation/village_enemy]

:::notice{type="info"}
Minecraft only sends unlocked advancement tabs to the client. A link cannot preview or jump to an advancement until its tab is available in your current world.
:::

Some entries are hidden challenges. Explore both helpful and harmful paths if you want the full tree—but expect villagers to remember the latter.
