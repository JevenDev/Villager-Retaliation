# Retaliation & Combat

Hitting a villager can anger the victim. Killing an adult in public can rally nearby adult witnesses when visibility rules match. Anger may expire while the lasting personal reputation damage remains.

Adult villagers can use melee weapons, bows, crossbows, tridents, shields, armor, potions, and profession-aware behavior. They may scavenge stronger equipment while threatened, preserve displaced gear when possible, ready carried Totems of Undying, and defend against hostile mobs according to server settings.

- Fletchers favor ranged attacks.
- Armorers and weaponsmiths are strong defenders.
- Clerics can heal and support allies and trusted players when configured.
- Babies are noncombatants; nitwits generally flee without a special combat reason.
- Same-party, allied-party, and same-village rules prevent many friendly combat mistakes.

## Environmental blame

Placed lava, flint and steel, and fire charges can attribute nearby fire or lava damage to the responsible player during a configurable window-two real-time minutes by default.

:::notice{type="danger"}
Ordinary villagers and hired workers can still die. Only an active protection rule enables the [downed state](combat/downed.md), and some damage bypasses it.
:::

For consensual combat, see [Villager Duels](combat/duels.md). For community-scale betrayal, see [Player Raids](combat/player-raids.md).
