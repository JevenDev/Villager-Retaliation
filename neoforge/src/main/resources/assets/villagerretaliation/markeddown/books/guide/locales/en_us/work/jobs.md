# Jobs

Most roles need a committed work area, reachable tools or stations, supplies, and output space. Combat, Hunting, and Courier can use routes. Nitwit needs no area; Builder uses its blueprint site.

:::details{id="combat-hunting" title="Combat and Hunting"}
- **Combat:** Weaponsmith, or Guarding + Archery. Guard protects an area or route; Roaming searches for natural hostiles.
- **Hunting:** Fletcher, or Archery + Survival. Hunts configured animals, hostiles, or players and collects drops. Enabling player targets creates real PvP risk.
:::

:::details{id="gathering-jobs" title="Mining, Logging, Farming, and Fishing"}
- **Mining:** Toolsmith, or Mining + Masonry. Needs an area and pickaxe; supports exposed ore and horizontal or vertical excavation.
- **Logging:** Gathering + Crafting. Needs an area and axe; can replant and clear leaves when configured and supplied.
- **Farming:** Farmer, or Farming + Gathering. Needs a hoe, seeds, and an area or claimed farmer job site.
- **Fishing:** Fisherman, or Fishing + Survival. Needs an area, rod, and accessible open water.
:::

:::details{id="processing-jobs" title="Brewing, Cooking, and Smelting"}
- **Brewing:** Cleric, or Medicine + Scholarship. Uses brewing stands, bottles, ingredients, and blaze powder for supported orders.
- **Cook:** Butcher, or Cooking + Gathering. Uses a furnace or smoker and, for supported foods such as bread, a crafting table. For bread, assign a Bread Recipe Filter and provide three wheat. Automatic work and continuous orders have a default 10% chance of one extra plain bread or eligible cooked food per operation. Exact batches, stock targets, and free lessons keep normal counts.
- **Smelter:** Armorer, or Smithing + Mining. Uses actual non-food furnace and blast-furnace recipes, including glass, stone, charcoal, and metals. Sand and stone processing require a normal furnace; blast furnaces accept only actual blasting recipes. Food remains Cook work. Recipe Filters select exact recipes within those station limits.

These automate supported outputs; they are not unrestricted recipe machines. Skill does not shorten vanilla station processing timers.
:::

:::details{id="specialized-jobs" title="Animal Handling, Courier, Nitwit, and Builder"}
- **Animal Handling:** Shepherd or Leatherworker, or Animal Handling + Farming. Breeds configured animals and collects products.
- **Courier:** every adult. Requires a usable route plus Input and Output storage.
- **Nitwit:** nitwit only. Produces occasional novelty work reports.
- **Builder:** Mason, or Masonry + Crafting. A separately paid one-off construction project.
:::

Successful measurable work can train a role's primary and support skills at a 70/30 split when growth is enabled. Read [Skills, attributes, and care](../life/skills-care.md) or [Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the special cases.
