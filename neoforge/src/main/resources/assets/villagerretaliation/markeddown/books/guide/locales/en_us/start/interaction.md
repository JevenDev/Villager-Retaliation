# Interaction & Dialogue

Use an **empty main hand** on a nearby adult :entity[minecraft:villager] to open the interaction screen. Sneak-use bypasses the screen for normal vanilla interaction by default.

## What can appear

- Conversation about greetings, stories, family, local events, home, and your relationship.
- Gifts, vanilla trade, and eligible Special Orders.
- Quest offers, reminders, choices, turn-ins, and abandonment.
- Hiring, job management, inventories, construction, recruitment, parties, and mounts when you have authority.

## Why an option is missing

The villager may have the wrong profession or trade level, lack a hidden skill or reputation requirement, be blocked by a prerequisite or cooldown, or currently be hired, recruited, sleeping, fighting, recovering, downed, too far away, or controlled by someone else.

:::details{id="dialogue-context" title="What dialogue remembers"}
Dialogue can react to personal reputation, profession, family, social attributes, mood, recent village events, first meetings, time apart, weather, equipment, nearby danger, stories, and quest history.

Villagers can choose different wording for the current context. Some lines are one-time or have durable per-player, per-villager, village, dimension, or world cooldowns, so repeating the same interaction does not guarantee the same response.

These are separate systems: **reputation** is persistent standing, **gossip** spreads witnessed information, **mood** is temporary, and **village allegiance** identifies community membership.
:::

:::notice{type="warning"}
A despised, hostile, or downed villager can refuse normal conversation or trade. Controlled states can also intentionally suppress vanilla trading.
:::
