The standard **Kingdoms: Villager Retaliation!** experience for your world.

This features all the generic quests and questlines without the added weight of specific storyline interactions, quests, or world events. This is great if you want a less intrusive experience, or if you're using another campaign pack. Other campaign packs can opt in to include this content, or entirely write their own.

Content included:

- Tutorial quests for jobs and settlements
- All dialogue options, ranging from basic interactions to specific conversation checks.
